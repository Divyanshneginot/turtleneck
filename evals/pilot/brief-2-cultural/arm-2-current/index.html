02 // CURATORIAL THESIS

Lithic Resonances

Architecture as acoustic instrument, infrasonic vessel, and planetary receiver.

“Architecture is neither inert nor silent. On an active volcanic rift, the lithosphere is an ongoing, deep-frequency lithophone. We do not build to impose sound; we construct cavities to decipher the internal respiration of the mantle.”
Dr. Astrid Lindqvist & Kaelen Mori Chief Curators // Archipelago 2027 (ETH Zürich & Tokyo University of the Arts)

The 2027 Biennial convenes 38 cross-disciplinary practices at the intersection of structural engineering, seismic geology, and spatialized acoustic composition. Across five unpopulated islands born of the same Mid-Atlantic tectonic fracture, each project occupies a direct thermodynamic relationship with its substrate.

The Caldera as Helmholtz Resonator: At Neá, architectural enclosures are tuned as passive cavities where oceanic tides compress ambient air masses, driving pneumatic infrasound organ columns below human audibility (12–19 Hz). Visitors experience sound not as ear-drum stimulus, but through tactile skeletal resonance.

Columnar Dolerite Waveguides: At Vardur, hexagonal crystalline basalt pillars naturally refract high-frequency acoustic waves. The interventions here reject synthetic speakers in favor of tensioned aeolian harps, wind-driven ceramic diaphragms, and seismic transducers that directly amplify the micro-tremors of magma chambers situated 4.2 kilometers beneath the sea floor.

1. Xenakis, I. (1971). Formalized Music: Thought and Mathematics in Composition. Chapter 4: Acoustic Spatial Enclosures.

2. Mori, K., & Lindqvist, A. (2025). Seismic Wave Propagation in Quenched Basalt Lithophones. Journal of Architectural Acoustics, Vol. 44, pp. 112–138.

3. Geological Datum: Real-time seismic telemetry provided by the Macaronesian Volcanological Institute (MVI). Infrasound installations adhere to ISO 226:2023 acoustic exposure guidelines.

05 // MARITIME LOGISTICS & PROTOCOL

Field Protocol

The archipelago is an active geodetic reserve. Visitors must comply with maritime safety schedules, decibel limits, and zero-trace ecological mandates.

⚓

Maritime Hydrofoils

Departures hourly from Puerto de las Nieves (07:30 to 20:00). Crossing duration: 22 to 44 minutes depending on sea swells. Vessel capacity is capped at 60 passengers per departure.

∿

Acoustic Sensitivity

Certain calderas operate infrasonic arrays below 20 Hz with high tactile sound pressure levels. Certified acoustic attenuator earplugs are provided complimentary upon embarkation.

▲

Volcanic Footwear

Razor-sharp quenched vesicular basalt and steep dolerite scree require ankle-height trekking boots with reinforced rubber soles. Soft trainers and open footwear are strictly prohibited.

◈

Zero-Trace Mandate

All calderas are protected nesting habitats for Macaronesian shearwaters. No commercial food packaging, artificial illuminations over 40 lumens, or civilian drones are permitted.
