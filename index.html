01 // The Architecture of Craft

Autonomous craft mandate.
Zero prompt wrestling.

You should never have to prompt "make it look nice" or "add states". Turtleneck operates on Turn 1 with principal design director standards.

Vernacular Harmony

100% commitment to ONE coherent design dialect. Zero aesthetic collage: no mixing editorial serifs with hacker terminals and pastel blur on one surface.

SYSTEM DIALECT: OBSIDIAN DEVTOOL
Unified border radii • Cohesive scale • Zero trope collision

Frequency-Gated Timing

0ms animation on high-frequency keyboard operations. 80–120ms atomic micro-interactions on buttons. Custom ease-out curves; never sluggish ease-in.

KEYBOARD ACTIONS:
0ms INSTANT

5-State Completeness

Every control declares default, hover, active (scale(0.97)), :focus-visible (2px solid, 2px offset), and disabled/loading states.

DEFAULT HOVER ACTIVE FOCUS

Layered Elevation Tokens

Tiered obsidian/slate surfaces with razor-sharp 1px inner borders. High optical depth without muddy glass blur or washed-out purple gradients.

SURFACE TIERS: #08090e → #10131d → #161a29

Authentic Domain Density

Real operational metrics, authentic domain models, and high-density state badges — never generic "Lorem ipsum", dummy graphs, or hollow card shells.

INGEST: 14,290 req/s p99: 14.2ms

Verified WCAG 2.2 AA

Mathematical contrast gate across every foreground/background token pair. Text ≥ 4.5:1, interactive boundaries ≥ 3.0:1. Zero illegible gray-on-gray.

Body Text: 18.6:1 AAA PASS
