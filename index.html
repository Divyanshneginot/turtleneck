Aesthetic Dissection

Slop vs. Calibrated Senior Craft

Every line of code either builds credibility or leaks laziness. Here is what Turtleneck strips out versus what it enforces.

Generic AI Slop Stereotype The Lazy Habit Turtleneck v2.0 Protocol Enforcement
Purple Glowing Blur Cards Arbitrary purple gradients and blurry glassmorphism that destroys contrast down to 2:1. Obsidian tiered elevation tokens (--canvas, --surface-1, --surface-2), razor-sharp 1px hairline borders, and specular rim highlights.
Cloned Server Telemetry Pasting Kubernetes cluster metrics (p99: 14ms, req/s) onto a municipal water treatment plant or dental clinic. Authentic domain empathy. Municipal plants receive real pump PSI, NTU turbidity, and chemical residual controls.
Bouncy 500ms Ease-In Sluggish animations on buttons, dropdowns, and tabs that delay user decisions. Frequency-gated timing. High-frequency actions trigger at 0ms; micro-interactions settle in 80–120ms with custom cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1).
Broken Mobile Viewports Squeezing 8-column wide desktop data tables into mobile screens, creating horizontal overflow and 16px tap targets. Strict mobile ergonomics. Verified 44x44px minimum touch targets, deliberate vertical stacking, and thumb-friendly controls.
Invisible Keyboard Focus Removing default focus outlines without replacing them, leaving keyboard and screen-reader users stranded. Non-negotiable :focus-visible rules with 2px solid outlines, 2px offset, and verified >=3:1 contrast across all surfaces.
